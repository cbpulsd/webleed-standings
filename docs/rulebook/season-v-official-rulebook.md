# Welcome to Season V

**Welcome to the weBLEEDfpv eSPORTS eSERIES Season V!**

Season V is our biggest and most competitive season yet, bringing together 50 of the world's best FPV pilots to compete in a six-week team-based championship on Velocidrone, the Official Drone Racing Simulator of MultiGP.

Pilots will compete across multiple environments using a different whoop nearly every week. The season rewards not only race-day performance but also consistency, teamwork, preparation, and leaderboard grinding.

Only the top qualifying pilots will earn a spot in the draft, where team captains will build ten five-pilot teams that will battle throughout the season before advancing to the Season V Championship.

Whether you're aiming to become a Team Drafter, earn a roster spot, or simply prove you're one of the fastest pilots in the simulator, we wish you the best of luck.

| **Now load up Velocidrone, grab your whoop, and SEND IT!** |

# 1. Season Overview

Season V consists of:

- Three official qualifying tracks
- One live draft
- Six weekly races
- One Championship Finale

## Season Structure

| **TEAMS** | **PILOTS PER TEAM** | **TOTAL PILOTS** |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 5 | 50 |

Every pilot drafted onto a team is expected to compete throughout the season while helping their team accumulate points toward the championship.

# 2. Registration & Entry Fee

**Participation in Season V requires a $50 USD entry fee.**

| **IMPORTANT** The entry fee is ONLY required if you are selected for the draft. |
|---|

Anyone may participate in qualifying.

After qualifying concludes, the Top 50 pilots will have five (5) days to purchase their Season V entry through:

**[www.weBLEEDfpv.com](http://www.webleedfpv.com/)**

Pilots who fail to purchase their entry within the allotted time will lose their roster spot.

Roster spots will then be offered to pilots on the waiting list in qualifying order.

Please do not participate in qualifying unless you are willing and able to pay the $50 entry fee if selected.

Once your registration has been completed, you are guaranteed to be drafted onto a team.

# 8. Weekly Race Format

Each Sunday consists of two phases.

## Phase One - Leaderboard Smash

Teams have until 11:59 PM EST Saturday to post their fastest leaderboard times. Once the clock strikes 11:59 PM EST we will export the Leaderboard from Velocidrone and this will determine the tiers going into the race.

All five pilots contribute to the team's leaderboard score.

## Phase Two - Race Night

- Four pilots from each team compete.
- One pilot must sit out.
- Each pilot must sit out exactly one race during the six-week season and one pilot will sit out twice.
- No pilot may race more than five weekly events.

Four rounds of racing will be completed.

Each round contains eight heats.

Pilots race only against pilots within their assigned tier.

| **TIER** | **MATCHUP** |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Tier 1 vs Tier 1 |
| Tier 2 | Tier 2 vs Tier 2 |
| Tier 3 | Tier 3 vs Tier 3 |
| Tier 4 | Tier 4 vs Tier 4 |
|  |  |

# 9. Team Scoring

Teams earn points in three different ways every week.

## A. Leaderboard Team Bonus

All five pilot leaderboard times are added together. The fastest combined team earns the highest bonus.

## B. Individual Heat Points

Every heat awards points using the standard 10-1 scoring system.

## C. Weekly Team Placement

After all races conclude, teams earn weekly placement points.

# 10. Bonus Bracket

Following the weekly races, the Top Four teams advance to the Bonus Bracket.

| **MATCH** | **TEAMS** |
|---|---|
| Match 1 | 1st vs 4th |
| Match 2 | 2nd vs 3rd |

- All four active pilots compete simultaneously.
- Each matchup consists of three heats.
- Heat points use the standard 8-1 scoring system.
- After three heats, total points determine the advancing teams.
- The winners meet in the Bonus Final.

## Bonus Points

| **PLACE** | **BONUS** |
|---|---|
| 1st | 3 |
| 2nd | 2 |
| 3rd | 1 |
| 4th | 0 |

# 11. Team Rules

- Every team consists of five pilots.
- Only four race each week.
- 4 of 5 pilots will race exactly 5 weeks where as 1 pilot will fly 4 weeks.
- Every pilot must sit out exactly one weekly race, and one pilot will sit out exactly two weekly races.
- Coordinate schedules carefully throughout the season.

# 12. Absences & Substitute Pilots

If a pilot has already used their required sit-out week but cannot race again, teams have two options.

## Option 1

Accept automatic last place for every heat in that tier.

## Option 2

Recruit a substitute.

Substitute pilots :

- Must have participated in Season V qualifying
- Must not have made the final Top 50
- Must have expressed interest in remaining available as substitutes

NOTE: There is only one single pilot that may be used as a substitute and that is: Eclipse

The substitute list will be provided by tournament organizers.

The same process applies if a pilot permanently leaves a team during the season.

# 13. Tie Breakers

Whenever a tie occurs in the weekly placement racing requiring advancement or placement determination, tournament staff will use a random number generator to select the tier that will compete in a race-off.

The selected tier from each tied team will race one deciding heat.

| **Winner advances.** |
|---|

In the event of a tie during the bonus round racing, we will utilize the Motocross rule meaning, whichever team scored higher in the weekly placement racing is the team who is awarded the win.

# 14. Championship Format

Every team qualifies for the Championship.

However, regular-season standings determine your starting position.

Higher-seeded teams receive a significant advantage.

## Championship Rules

All five pilots must:

- Be present.
- Have submitted a leaderboard time.
- Compete in their assigned relay.

There are:

- No exceptions.
- No substitutes.

**ELIGIBILITY:** Any pilot who fails to submit a championship leaderboard time is ineligible to race.

## Relay Format

The Championship consists of two relay races. All five pilots must compete, with each pilot assigned to exactly one relay.

| **RELAY** | **TIERS** | **PILOTS PER TEAM** | **TOTAL TEAM LAPS** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier 5 + Tier 4 | 2 | 10 |
| 2 | Tier 3 + Tier 2 + Tier 1 | 3 | 15 |

**Pilot Requirements:**

- Every pilot must complete exactly five laps.
- Pilots must tag in their teammates through the designated recharge gate.
- Every pilot competes in only one relay.
- Each team's total completion time is recorded for both relays.

## Championship Scoring

The Championship is determined by combined completion time, not points.

- Each team's completion time from Relay 1 and Relay 2 will be combined.
- The lowest combined time determines the winning team for that round.
- The highest combined time receives a strike and is eliminated.
- All teams competing in a round must complete both relays before elimination is determined.

**TIME-BASED SCORING:** The combined time from both relays determines each team's overall standing. There are no points awarded for finishing positions.

## Championship Elimination

Teams enter the Championship based on their final regular-season standings. The Championship follows a progressive elimination format, with the next highest-seeded team entering after each elimination.

A maximum of three teams may compete in any championship round.

| **ROUND** | **STARTING TEAMS / SEEDS** | **RESULT** |
|---|---|---|
| Round One | 10th, 9th, and 8th Seeds | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 10th overall. |
| Round Two | Remaining two teams + 7th Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 9th overall. |
| Round Three | Remaining two teams + 6th Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 8th overall. |
| Round Four | Remaining two teams + 5th Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 7th overall. |
| Round Five | Remaining two teams + 4th Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 6th overall. |
| Round Six | Remaining two teams + 3rd Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 5th overall. |
| Round Seven | Remaining two teams + 2nd Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 4th overall. |
| Round Eight | Remaining two teams + 1st Seed | Slowest team eliminated and finishes 3rd overall. |
| Championship Final | Top Two Remaining Teams | Both teams compete one final time. Lowest combined relay time becomes the **weBLEEDfpv eSPORTS eSERIES Season V Champion!** |

# 15. Pilot Requirements

Every pilot is responsible for:

- Flying every required whoop
- Qualifying on all three tracks
- Using Single Class or Nemesis Mode
- Racing exactly five weekly races minus one pilot flying exactly four weekly races
- Sitting out one weekly race (One pilot sitting out two weekly races)
- Posting leaderboard times every week
- Attending the Championship
- Communicating with your team

| **Failure to meet these requirements may negatively impact your team.** |
